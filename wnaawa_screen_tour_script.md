# Wnaawa Website Screen-Tour Advertisement

## Production direction

Create a moving screen-tour advertisement captured from the Wnaawa website itself. The camera should glide through the real interface rather than display disconnected stills. Use smooth zooms, cursor-like focus moves, short scrolls, and gold callout highlights around the points balance, navigation, rewards, store filters, PC service tiers, and ticket actions. Keep the visual language dark, glassmorphic, and gold-accented.

## Tour sequence

| Scene | Website surface | Story beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Overview / Home | Introduce Wnaawa as a clear digital service center built around useful support and meaningful rewards. |
| 2 | Points balance and ledger | Show that the starting balance is zero and that verified points are tracked transparently. |
| 3 | Rewards | Demonstrate the optional ad session and explain that points are added only after verification; the timer alone never grants points. |
| 4 | Store filters | Move through All, Points, and BOT & Website filters to explain that every request follows a visible payment rule. |
| 5 | PC service tiers | Highlight Starter, Creator, and Performance PC categories as accessible options for different needs and budgets, using advertising points where eligible. |
| 6 | Request flow | Show selecting a service, entering WhatsApp contact details, choosing points or an eligible cash method, and creating a ticket. |
| 7 | Admin / ticket delivery | Show that the request becomes a real ticket and reaches the Wnaawa operations channel for follow-up. |
| 8 | Closing CTA | End on Wnaawa's mission and the website URL, with a clear availability and terms disclaimer. |

## English voiceover

**Style:** English (US), warm, sincere, confident documentary-commercial voice. Speak clearly, with a human and hopeful tone, never implying guaranteed rewards, guaranteed delivery, or guaranteed outcomes.

Welcome to Wnaawa — a digital service center designed to turn limited budgets into meaningful opportunities.

Start with a clear overview of your balance, your activity, and the services available to you. Every account begins at zero, and every point is tracked through a transparent ledger.

When eligible rewards are available, watch an optional ad experience. Points are added only after the network verifies completion. No timer alone can change your balance.

Then open the Store. Browse every service, filter by Points, or explore BOT and Website services with their clearly stated payment rules.

Looking for a computer? Choose the PC category that fits your goal. Starter for everyday work and study. Creator for editing, streaming, and design. Performance for demanding workloads and serious play.

Select a service, enter your WhatsApp contact, choose the allowed payment method, and create your request. Your ticket is saved and delivered to the Wnaawa operations channel for follow-up.

Wnaawa is built to help people whose financial circumstances make essential digital services harder to reach — with a mission centered on practical support and helping people move closer to their goals.

For services that offer delivery, requests can reach customers internationally, including the Middle East and Asia, subject to availability, verification, and service terms.

Wnaawa. Useful services. Meaningful rewards. Open the site, explore your options, and put your points to work.
