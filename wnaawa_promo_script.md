# Wnaawa Promotional Concept

## Creative direction

Create a premium 30–40 second digital service advertisement in the Wnaawa visual language: near-black background, glassmorphism surfaces, restrained metallic gold, aurora green depth, and a geometric folded-W service mark. The story should make the points economy feel simple: users watch eligible ads, earn advertising points when verified, and use those points to request PC services in clear tiers. Do not promise guaranteed rewards, cash value, delivery times, specifications, or prices that are not confirmed. Use “availability varies” language.

## PC service tiers shown on screen

| Tier | Positioning | Payment language |
|---|---|---|
| Starter PC | Entry-level setup for everyday work, study, browsing, and lightweight play. | Request with points; availability varies. |
| Creator PC | Balanced setup for editing, streaming, design, and heavier multitasking. | Request with points; availability varies. |
| Performance PC | Higher-performance configuration for demanding workloads and gaming. | Request with points; availability varies. |
